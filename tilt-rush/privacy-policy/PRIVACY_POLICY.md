# Privacy Policy for Tilt Rush

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Tilt Rush is a mobile game developed by Marco Barbone ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how Tilt Rush handles user data.

## Data We Collect

Tilt Rush may process the following data:

- Gameplay data such as scores and run history stored locally on the device.
- Google Play Games Services data, such as your Play Games player ID, display name, authentication status, and leaderboard score submissions, if you choose to sign in.
- App diagnostics and analytics data through Firebase, such as crash reports, device/app version information, and usage events, when Firebase services are enabled in the app build.
- Advertising data processed by Google AdMob and its ad partners, which may include the Android advertising ID, other device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data.

## How We Use Data

We use data to:

- Save your local game progress and score history.
- Submit leaderboard scores and open the Google Play Games leaderboard.
- Diagnose crashes and improve game stability and gameplay quality.
- Measure basic gameplay usage and feature performance.
- Display and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted by your choices and applicable law.

## Data Sharing

Tilt Rush may share limited data with third-party service providers used by the app:

- Google Play Games Services, for authentication and leaderboard features.
- Firebase Analytics, for app analytics.
- Firebase Crashlytics, for crash reporting and diagnostics.
- Google AdMob and its advertising partners, for serving and measuring ads.

We do not sell personal data.

## Data Storage

- Local score history is stored on your device.
- If Android backup is enabled on your device, some local app data may be backed up by Android.
- Leaderboard-related data submitted through Google Play Games is processed by Google.
- Analytics and crash diagnostics sent through Firebase are processed by Google.
- Advertising data sent through AdMob is processed by Google and its ad partners.

## Data Retention

- Local game data remains on your device until you remove the app data or uninstall the app.
- Data processed by Google Play Games, Firebase, or AdMob is retained according to those services' policies and settings.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Choose whether to sign in to Google Play Games.
- Stop using leaderboard features by not signing in to Google Play Games.
- Clear local app data through your device settings.
- Uninstall the app at any time.
- Where required, review your ad privacy choices in the consent form or the in-app privacy options.

## Children's Privacy

Tilt Rush is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to reduce unnecessary data collection and use platform services for leaderboard, analytics, and crash reporting.
